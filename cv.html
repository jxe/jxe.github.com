Joe Edelman

Berlin · nxhx.org · joe@meaningalignment.org @edelwax · GitHub · Google Scholar

Current

  • 2023–present Meaning Alignment Institute Co-founder & Principal Investigator Lead research on thick models of value, moral graph elicitation, full-stack alignment, and post-market and democratic mechanisms. Principal investigator on the Full-Stack Alignment research program, a network of 50+ researchers at universities and industry labs. Collaborators include Tan Zhi-Xuan, Matija Franklin, Iason Gabriel, Jakob Foerster, Oliver Klingefjord, Ryan Lowe, Seth Lazar, etc.

Previous Positions

  • 2007–2009 CouchSurfing International Reputation Systems & Metrics Joined when the site had ~100k active users. Developed the meaning-based organizational metrics that became the north star for the company and the primary criterion for feature rollouts, tracking tens of millions of in-person hosting experiences. Built the reputation and trust system, including ML models for user safety. [metrics writeup]

  • 2013–2015 Center for Humane Technology Co-founder Co-founded CHT with Tristan Harris. Led research on the design of healthier social-media ecosystems. Coined “Time Well Spent,” a framing and family of metrics subsequently adopted by teams at Facebook, Google, and Apple. Published mockups and designs for user-protection interventions that closely prefigured what later shipped as iOS Screen Time and Downtime. Briefed policymakers on political polarization, social isolation, declining trust, and internet addiction. Departed when the research track was moved outside CHT's scope.

  • 2015–2020 Independent Researcher Funded by Patrick Collison and Stripe. Delivered the influential talk “Is Anything Worth Maximizing?”, which led to recommender system changes at Facebook, Apple, and Google. Developed the philosophical foundations of new business metrics and design methods.

  • 2017–2022 School for Social Design Founder Online training for humane recommender systems. Trained product, metrics, and UX practitioners from Google, Facebook, Twitter, Notion, and other companies in values-based data science.

Workshops Organized

  • AGI-Ready Institution Design, Paris, February 2026. Co-located with IASEAI. Convened ~25 researchers from DeepMind, Oxford, UC Berkeley, ANU, GovAI, Mila, the Cooperative AI Foundation, and others across mechanism design, social choice, legal theory, political philosophy, and AI governance.
