## Previous Positions

- 2007–2009 **CouchSurfing International** *Reputation Systems & Metrics*
  Joined when the site had ~100k active users. Developed the meaning-based organizational metrics that became the north star for the company and the primary criterion for feature rollouts, tracking tens of millions of in-person hosting experiences. Built the reputation and trust system, including ML models for user safety. [[metrics writeup]](https://nxhx.org/csmetrics/)

- 2013–2015 **Center for Humane Technology** *Co-founder*
  Co-founded CHT with Tristan Harris. Led research on the design of healthier social-media ecosystems. Coined “Time Well Spent,” a framing and family of metrics subsequently adopted by teams at Facebook, Google, and Apple. Published mockups and designs for user-protection interventions that closely prefigured what later shipped as iOS Screen Time and Downtime. Briefed policymakers on political polarization, social isolation, declining trust, and internet addiction. Departed when the research track was moved outside CHT's scope.

- 2015–2020 **Independent Researcher**
  Funded by Patrick Collison and Stripe. Delivered the influential talk “Is Anything Worth Maximizing?”, which led to recommender system changes at Facebook, Apple, and Google. Developed the philosophical foundations of new business metrics and design methods.

- 2017–2022 **School for Social Design** *Founder*
  Online training for humane recommender systems. Trained product, metrics, and UX practitioners from Google, Facebook, Twitter, Notion, and other companies in values-based data science.
