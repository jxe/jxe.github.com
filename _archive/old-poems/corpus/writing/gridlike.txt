On certain days, the world groans
like an astronaut settling back into gravity
or a cop with more paperwork to do.
The air is ribbed with tight percussive clouds
and indeed there is a gridlike quality to everything,
an orderly reminiscence that speaks to imperfection.

On these days the architects hunch at their drafting boards
with lower-back pain and aching muscles,
and their visions look like things made of pencil lead not concepts.

On these days men and women sleep separately in the same beds
and friendly smiles are ignored,
and sex is exhausting and difficult
and the telephone system works perfectly.
