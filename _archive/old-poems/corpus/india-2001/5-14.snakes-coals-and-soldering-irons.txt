I had just written this:

"All my ITEM# notes so far have been about something I did right or
figured out.  It's about time I wrote about something I've done wrong.

"I'm in Haridwar, sitting about six feet away from the holy Ganges.
People are bathing around me and I am an uninvited unbeliever with
a laptop.  Some people stare at me with distaste, a few try to sell
me drugs.  I will have no alternative tonight but to stay in some lame
expensive hotel for outsiders like myself.  If I had planned ahead,
done some research, made an inquiry in one of the many ashrams here
ahead of time, I maybe could have come as a student.  Even if I had come
with another person, it might be possible to have some adventure here,
but all alone I feel dead and unwanted."

...when I was approached by a pothead baba who suggested I take a dip
in the Ganges and wash away my nasty attitude and my travel smell.
I did as he suggested and shortly began a very involved adventure:

After spending most of the day with the pothead baba, who allowed himself
to be bit by a cobra and burned himself with hot coals and then tried to
get the cobra to bite *me* and tried to burn *me* with the same coals,
I was sheltered by a poorer-than-poor family of five who took me into
their 3ft by 5ft tent made of plastic bags and bamboo and fed me chapati
(flour & water, toasted) and okra, which is the cheapest vegetable matter
around here.  Mom (who looked about 25) and the 10 year old girl took a
liking to me, and mom collected some interesting seeds from the ground
nearby and taught me their hindi names while the girl made funny faces
and taught me hindi for nose (nak) and lip (tik).

When the baba found me again and tried to burn me I left for the city
where I immediately ran into the most unsavory character I've met yet
in my travels:  a homosexual pimp who, when I explained that I wasn't
interested in paying for sex with either men or women, tried to hire me
(he offered US$40,000 a month!) to send friends of mine in the U.S. to
work as call-girls under fake visas in India, possibly trapped and
without pay.  The odd thing is that, when I explained that I probably
wasn't interested in *that either*, he said that I probably didn't want to
stay in his hotel, which is a brothel, but that he would find me another.
He introduced me to a motherly old woman who gave me a very good room
for a very good price and offered to take me to her ashram tomorrow,
which has an associated school and hospital.  So now I don't know whether
to report the guy to the authorities, or whether the motherly old woman
and her ashram is really some kind of front, or what.

But I really like the room, and the other guests are these upscale
english-speaking indian types.  I just met a really very attractive
indian girl, perhaps 20 years old, who's enrolled in a five-year B of S
/ masters degree program for electrical engineering.  She was wearing
a very traditional dress, almost fully covered, with those Bejoran
connected earrings, and I tried to imagine her soldering a breadboard,
but I could not.

***

Every westerner I met at the ashram was so insecure it was inappropriate
in some way:  one middle-aged American woman who had been there for
eight years expressed, within our first 10 minutes together, that it
might have been better if she had stayed in America and got married.
But for all their lack of sureness in conversations with me, they felt
sure enough of themselves in assigning arbitrary exercises to the kids
in the school they ran:  in computer class, the homework for today is
to draw a picture of a computer and label the parts (keyboard, monitor,
CPU, printer)... disgusting.  And they seem to disrespect the kids even
more than they disrespect themselves.

But I think it's harder to fool yourself into thinking you're healing
people than educating them, so the hospital they run seems alright.

I did what I could to help in both places.  The kids liked me a lot,
probably because I took them seriously.

Onward!
