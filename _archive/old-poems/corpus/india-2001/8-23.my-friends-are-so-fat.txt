Item 8-23:  My friends are so fat, I mean cool.

The day before I left Dharamsala it was raining.  I didn't feel like rain,
so I grabbed my umbrella and some snack food and walked up the mountain,
above the cloud ceiling, to where it was sunny.  That was (I think) about
12,000 feet.  It's nice when you can do something about rain.

So I found a nice cliff to sit on, and I sat there and dried off, when I
began to wish I had a chocolate bar.  Sometimes families that live in the
hills resell chocolate to get some extra dough.  In very little time I
found one, and along with my Cadbury dark chocolate they gave me... the Feb
2001 issue of National Geographic.  I don't know how they happened to have
it.  So I sat again in this fantastic place, watching it rain on the
lowlands, amidst the highest mountains in the world, feeling pretty spiff,
and leafing through the magazine...

When I notice that it's about two of my friends:  the two cover stories in
the Feb 01 National Geographic are about work on NASA's Mars Rover project
(Marty Vona) and climactic analysis of ice cores in Antarctica (Katie
Catapano).  40 pages of coverage, in total.

I looked around me.

"We're all on mountains," I said.  "All of us, glorious, sitting on the
tallest mountains in the world."
