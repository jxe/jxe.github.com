NOTES

(these are from the little notebook where, when I'm feeling down, I write
revolutionary slogans for myself)

I declare to everyone that life is a secret adventure to heal the world.

I don't want to find meaning in life, I want meaning to be thrust upon me--
to jump out at me from behind rocks and cliffs and people and adventures.

It seems to me there are only three attitudes I should try to adopt: the
theraputic, the adversarial, and the sublime.  The theraputic concerns the
healing of the world and of the objects, people, and places within it, as
well as the artistic completion and wholeness of things.  The adversarial
concerns surprises, gifts, resistence against clutter and habit and
advertising, as well as dexterity, play, and exultation.  The sublime is
like both of these, or neither, or nothing at all, or something else
entirely.

(clearly I've been spending too much time inside)


LISA

It's been more than a month since I've seen or talked with Lisa, so I might
get this wrong.

Quantum physicists talk about the business and bustle that fills a vacuum.
Aparently when you empty space of all matter and energy, due to some
quantum laws, that space becomes full of probabilities and fluctuations--
momentary glimpses of things, etc.

Lisa is like this.  So am I, I think.  The wonderful thing about Lisa is
the way she's really kind of empty, and so everything fills her up.  The
way she is so totally possessed and overwhelmed while watching the sunset,
or the melodrama that fills her up while listening to melodramatic music--
while listening to Bjork.

Lisa doesn't have any ideas.  She disrespects her own ideas, is empty of
ideas, and something wonderful comes from all around and fills her up.
Something fills her up with melodrama and admiration, and she throws
herself into things, feelings, and people, seeing only the object not
herself.  She's filled with melodrama and admiration, which are like the
things that I'm filled with.  So I think she might understand my desires
for adventure, surprises, conviviality, and peace.

Somehow I feel like most people's emotions are alien to me, but like Lisa's
emotions might make sense.  Somehow I feel like Lisa and I have an
adventure in store for us.

And another thing about Lisa:  most people seem to think you have to
empathize with yourself before you can empathize with the world, but Lisa
came to understand herself by appreciating and watching the things around her.
