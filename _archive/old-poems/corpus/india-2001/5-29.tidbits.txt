SOME GOOD THINGS TO DO WHILE WAITING IN INDIA

in the post office / train station / etc:

	1. Play with your face.  2. Reconnect with the infinite.
	3. Calculate Pi in your head using the pin throwing method.
	4. Imagine crazy conversations between the people near you in
	line.  5. Try to discover ways in which you can imagine moving
	your hands but in which you cannot actually move them.	6. Flirt.


DUSTIN

Dustin, the Canadian we had all disrespected in our own way-- Dustin,
the Pearl Jam block-chord "let's jam" guitarist stuck in the oversized
all-star body of a 19-year old high-school wrestler-- Dustin, the
young man on a spiritual search which seemed more like a search for pot
and a need to argue about almost everything.  Dustin-- this Dustin--
left before dawn.  His stuff disappeared from all of our rooms at some
time between 4 and 5am.  None of us woke.  And so Dustin, who had never
managed to impress us with words or music, impressed us with the simple
act of rising early and with stealth.


DELHI

New Delhi is very much like Washington, D.C.  Expecting the third world, I
was surprised to find broad, well-paved streets, rolling landscaped lawns,
halogen street lamps, and monuments and imposing buildings replete with
pillars, both doric and ionic, and floodlights.  All of this is arrayed
in an orderly, spoke-and-wheel fashion, and as I was riding in the cab,
amazed by this similarity to the west and to my own country, it was only
very slowly that I came to realize the principal difference.  On the
roofs of all these public buildings, atop their ornate but generally flat
upper surfaces, there were people-- thousands of people, some standing,
some sleeping on wooden beds.

"So that's where they put the extra population!" I said to myself.


THE MANGO

The Mango, like undivided white light, contains every fruit.  Before Babel
fell and men began to speak of "apple", "orange" & "cranberry", there was
only Mango, and it was variety itself.	Mangoes come in every texture,
from crunchy apple to soft melon to fleshy apricot, and there are red
mangoes, golden mangoes, green mangoes.  The Guru Garanth Sahib speaks
of a blue mango, visible only to initiates.  The Babas of Rishikesh say
that, when they eat of mango, they become like Shiva, the destroyer,
who also ate of mango.	Scientists at Los Alamos have discovered that,
in the split second after a nuclear explosion, a mango can be said to
exist at the epicenter, having definite position but indefinite momentum;
and Gauss' law predicts that the ideal magnetic field of a rotating
dipole is mango shaped.

Furthermore, I have a mango in my ear.
