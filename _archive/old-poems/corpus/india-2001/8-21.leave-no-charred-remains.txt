ARRIVAL
        
I fishtailed into Varanasi going 300kph in a stolen Tata Sumo, the Delhi
police still too close for comfort.  I handed my keys to a lucky
chai-wallah and hopped a ferry to the far side of the Ganges, where I
planned to disappear for a few days.

um...

I am an invisible black dot falling through the black humid night of
Varanasi.  At 4am, only my Chinese contact and a streetsweeper are awake to
see me strike earth and collect my parachute near the main ghat.  The
Chinese, who identifies himself as CO2 (cee oh two), takes me to a "safe
house", where I am armed and briefed.

err...

I was in my reconnaisance submarine, the Excelsior, drifting down the
Ganges at a depth of 20 meters.  Upon reaching Varanasi I activated the
"cremated skeleton" disguise mode and I was able to surface without being
noticed.  It had been only a few minutes when my radar began to sound the
alarm:  three MIGs, coming fast!

or...

I fell out of the train at Varanasi Junction with a dangerous gleam in my
eye:  awake and unwashed for almost three days, I was ready to kill for a
shower.  My lawyers won't let me tell what happened, but suffice it to say
that within forty minutes, cleaned and stowed, I napped on my newfound bed.



BURNINGMAN / BURNING GHAT

Joe has declared his own Burning Man Festival on the Manikarnika Ghat in
Varanasi, where real (dead) men and women are burning and there are several
weird sculptures.  For the week of Aug26, he will be buying and giving away
fruit at this BURNING GHAT FESTIVAL and he will be trying to engage the
Hindi friends and relatives of the recently deceased in modern dance.

He is very interested in multimedia collaborations & especially in the
possibility of a video wall connecting BurningMan West (Black Rock City) and
BurningMan East (Varanasi).

He is also offering a free service where you can email him a prayer or wish
and he'll write it on a scrap of paper, try to get it burned with a good
looking corpse, and send you a picture.

It smells like a texas bar-b-que here.

        Keshari Restaurant, Dasaswamedh, Varanasi
