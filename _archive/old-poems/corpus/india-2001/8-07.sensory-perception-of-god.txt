THE SENSORY PERCEPTION OF GOD

GOD is a warm quality in the light of the coldest most dreary day, and a
thing like silence amidst the noisiest crowd.  God is in difference tones,
resultants, and unexpected harmonics.  God is in the way everything dances,
in the way every tree limb and leaf waves softly or vigorously in the wind,
in a vast & cosmic dance.


THE LOGICAL ORDERING OF THE ESCHATON

I don't think God created the universe.  I think the universe was created,
probably by scientific means, and once it was created it discovered that it
wanted to be glorious, that being finite it wanted to seem infinite, and
that it wanted to dance and move around in exaltation of its pervasive law,
and that it wanted to speak.  So I think that the universe created God.
Everywhere.  All at once.
