
NON-VIOLENCE & COOKIE DOUGH

Every day I find I grow more similar to my parents.  For instance, my mom
is a teacher and I have just recently discovered that I really like
teaching.  Or, for another example, my dad has really bad knees, and the
day before yesterday I tore or distended a ligament on the outside of my
right knee.  The German doctor caused me intense pain, agreed with my own
diagnosis, told me to put ice on it, and gave me an ace bandage and a
crutch.  The Tibetan doctor asked me to move it around a bit, said that one
of my energy channels was blocked, told me to keep it warm, and gave me
some chocolate chip cookie dough to cover it with.  He was upfront about it
being cookie dough.  "Cookie dough is good for this sort of thing," he
said.

One thing they both agreed on was that it wasn't a very serious rip or
distension (or energy block), so it would heal.

If I can keep myself from licking the cookie dough off my leg.


Incidentally I injured my knee and lost my wallet all within 20 minutes
during sunset two nights ago.  And an ex-Hari Krishna had warned me to lock
my door and stay inside during sunset that night because strange and evil
things were going to happen.

I saw him on the way to the hospital.  He said "I told you so."

When I get my strength back I'm going to find him and kick his ass.


All of the dancers and buddhist monks I teach are coming to visit me,
though, and bringing me food, and that's pretty cool.

Maybe they'll help me beat up this ex-Hari Krishna.  I bet my gang of
buddhists and dancers could really do a number on him.



THE WEBSITE

Thanks to the people (mom, rob, jbrown) who tried it out and wrote me.
Your comments were a great help.  All of the errors you reported (joe &
rob) have been fixed.  In a day or two I should have a new version which
allows the person that made a well to delete wishes in it, lets users
search for a well that's closest to a given latitude & longitude, and makes
wish authors only visible to those who wish for the same thing (for rob).

I have a few questions for y'all, though:

  * A couple of you had difficulty with the idea that wells are places.  Do
    you think I should change the city/well interface?  Could I demand that
    a person entering a new well upload a picture of that well, so that
    people realize more that it's a real place?  Should I try for some kind
    of mapping function or something?  What should I do here?

  * The site only really starts working when people use the "mark as"
    button to assert that they share a wish with another person.  Then the
    "my.friends" page lights up and they can start thinking about how to
    collaborate with that person.  But few people have used this button.
    How should I make this more obvious?

  * I think maybe the idea of making wishes isn't compelling enough to
    make people visit the site since only three people did, and one of them
    was my mother.  My eventual hope is that the site (a) becomes a
    beautiful collection of wishes, (b) serves to connect people similiar
    or compatible wishes and people with both more and less experience, (c)
    can serve as a knowledge & interest management system for friend groups
    and small organizations, and (d) makes some places in the real world
    more significant or sacred than they already are.  So how can I
    repackage this wish thing as a more compelling thing, so that people
    will actually visit it.  Is it possible?  Am I a loon?

Thank you.

      Joe
