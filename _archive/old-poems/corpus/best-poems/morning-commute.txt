Traveling back & forth
beneath the world
on tracks

traveling to a small box
from a small box
in a small box

with windows that
don't actually pass light

I want to recall
that the world
is beautiful;
that things can be done here

I want to look out my windshield
and see

a six year old who knows better
than to do what I do
an eight year old with eyes
like secret violet fire

I hear there are poets and scientists
trying to find the truth
but I see no evidence of them
on my morning commute
