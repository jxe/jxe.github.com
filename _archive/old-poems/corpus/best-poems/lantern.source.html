A Lantern

A lantern is hung by some rope, knotted right,
and a steel nail driven into the wall.
That nail was heated before it was shaped--
You know it's ready when it glows and sparks.
A knot means a rope passed through itself,
holding itself to itself, and away.
A rope is overlapping fibers braided.
A good rope grows tighter with use.
A good lantern's adjustable
and lights just the things you want to see.
A nail driven right and the wood makes room for it
orthogonal to the grain,
and you can hang a lantern with it,
by some rope,
and it'll stay there.
