# Old poems editorial archive

This directory preserves the editorial workspace and source corpus behind the public
[`/old-poems/`](../../old-poems/) reader. It is deliberately kept under `_archive/`
so GitHub Pages does not publish the unselected and working material.

The current manuscript is **The Crab Is All: Prose poems from India, 2001**. Its title, selection, order, and edits are all provisional. It is a first way of seeing the material, not a final verdict.

## Structure

- `../../old-poems/index.html` — the public interactive reader grouped by year, sequence, and context
- `manuscript.md` — the current, readable book draft
- `selection-notes.md` — the reasoning behind the first shortlist and the pieces being held back
- `corpus/india-2001/` — all 25 texts from `india.zip`, with email transport headers removed but original wording and line wrapping retained
- `corpus/india-2001/SOURCE.md` — provenance and transcription notes
- `inbox/` — landing place for the poems still to come from Dropbox

## Editorial principles

1. Preserve the source before revising it.
2. Select for the book, not merely for individual quality.
3. Prefer pieces in which attention, surprise, and thought become action or image.
4. Treat dated cultural language and portrayals of identifiable people as publication questions, not as invisible copyedits.
5. Keep cuts, retitling, correction, and rewriting visible in the notes until Joe approves them.
